About Me

With over 20 years of leadership experience, I have built my career on optimizing complex operations and guiding teams through data-driven decision-making. My transition into software development is an evolution of this expertise, merging a veteran's perspective on project management with a deep focus on building resilient, user-centered digital systems.

While my professional focus is on Front-End Development, my technical curiosity extends to the hardware level. I manage a five-node Proxmox cluster where I architect environments utilizing virtualization, automated power management, and secure networking. This allows me to engineer interfaces that are not only responsive and accessible but deeply informed by the infrastructure they inhabit.
