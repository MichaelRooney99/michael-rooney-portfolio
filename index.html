About Me

In April 2026, an email from my company's Network Engineering Manager turned "learning to code on the side" into a real decision with a real deadline. I'd spent 20 years in operations leadership — optimizing workflows, running teams, making data-driven calls under pressure — and that conversation was the push to point all of it at software and infrastructure instead.

While my professional focus is on Front-End Development, my technical curiosity extends to the hardware level. I manage a five-node Proxmox cluster where I architect environments utilizing virtualization, automated power management, and secure networking. This allows me to engineer interfaces that are not only responsive and accessible but deeply informed by the infrastructure they inhabit.

How Leadership Became Practice

20 Years of Operational Planning → Every feature gets a written scope doc before any code
Compliance & Safety Standards → Ansible playbooks with tagged tasks, verify steps, and audit runs
Data-Driven Decision Making → Query the real source first — raw PromQL, curl, upsc — before trusting logs or UI
Team Recruitment & Growth → Documentation as a deliverable, not an afterthought — every project ships with a journal
